Who receives it

  • Supabase: database and servers, in the EU.
  • RevenueCat: handling purchases and subscriptions.
  • Google (AdMob): ads. With your consent, personalised ads.
  • Apple and Google: payments, sign-in and app distribution.
  • Expo and Google Firebase: delivering notifications.

Some of these companies are in the US. Where data leaves the EU, we rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses. We never sell your data.

Your choices and rights

You can reopen your ad choices at any time in Settings. Saying no still shows ads, just less personal ones, and rewarded gold keeps working.

You can ask for access to your data, correct it, delete it, object to some uses, move it to another service, or withdraw consent. In the app, go to Settings › Privacy, data & play responsibly for Download my data and Delete account. You can also ask for deletion here.

You can complain to the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection in Cyprus.

Age

Trapoula is for adults 18 and over only. If we learn a player is under 18, we close the account.

Guests

A guest account's progress and purchases stay on that device until you link Apple or Google.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app.
